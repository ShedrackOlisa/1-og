Career Mode — More Depth Than You'd Expect

I'll admit, I didn't expect much from FTS's career mode. Most lightweight football games treat career mode as an afterthought. Throw in some matches, maybe a transfer window, call it a day. But FTS 26 surprised me. You negotiate contracts — actually negotiate, with wage demands and signing bonuses. You manage staff — fitness coaches, scout networks, medical teams. You deal with player morale and form. It's not as deep as Football Manager, but it's way deeper than I expected for a 350MB game. I've gone through four seasons with a created club, and I'm genuinely invested in my youth academy products.

Create-a-Player — Dangerously Addictive

I have a confession. I spent three hours creating my Sunday league team in FTS 26. All 25 players. With accurate (embarrassing) stats. The create-a-player system is surprisingly robust — you can customize everything from appearance to preferred foot to celebration style. The best part? You can put custom teams into any league. I replaced all the Premier League teams with amateur clubs from my local area. Watching my buddy's team (rated 65 overall) battle Man City (rated 87) is hilarious. I lost 7-0, but the journey was worth it. The mod also includes all the real players from the 2026 season, so you can mix and match.

Gameplay — The Right Balance of Sim and Arcade

This is where FTS really shines. The controls are simple enough for anyone to pick up, but there's enough depth for skilled players to excel. Passing requires aiming, not just tapping. Shooting timing matters — too early and you'll sky it, too late and it's blocked. Skill moves have been expanded this year — step-overs, feints, and even a rainbow flick if you're feeling fancy. The pace is faster than simulation games but slower than pure arcade. It's in that sweet spot where matches feel exciting but not unrealistic. The AI adapts to your playstyle. If you keep spamming through balls, they'll drop deeper. If you play possession, they'll press higher. It's genuinely impressive for a game of this size.
