Why FIFA 14 Still Holds Up in 2025

The ball physics felt realistic, you could take heavy touches, misplace passes, and feel the difference between players. The career mode had depth, you could scout youth players, negotiate contracts, and deal with player morale. The graphics were crisp without being overly demanding. The mod takes this foundation and updates everything for 2025. All the kits are current. The squads reflect the latest transfers. Even the bench players are accurate.
