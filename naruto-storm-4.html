Combat — Fast, Flashy, and Strategic

The combat system is deep but accessible. Light attack, heavy attack, chakra dash, substitution jutsu, guard, and awakening. The substitution jutsu lets you teleport behind an opponent at the cost of chakra. The chakra dash allows you to close distance quickly. The awakening mode transforms your character — Naruto enters Kurama mode, Sasuke unlocks his Susanoo, and Kakashi uses his Sharingan. The PSP port preserves the combat system, though the controls are simplified. The frame rate is solid during 1v1 matches but can drop during team battles. A controller is highly recommended.

The story mode is the best in the series. You'll fight through the war as Naruto, Sasuke, and the other Kage. The cutscenes are high-quality, with voice acting from the original Japanese cast (or English dubs).
