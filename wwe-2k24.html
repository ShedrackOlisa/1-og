Installation Guide

This is a large download. Make sure you have enough storage:

Step 1: Download PPSSPP from the Google Play Store (free version works perfectly).
Step 2: Download the WWE 2K24 ISO using the button below (about 3.2GB — this will take a while).
Step 3: If it's a ZIP file, extract it using ZArchiver or any file manager.
Step 4: Open PPSSPP, tap "Games", and navigate to the folder containing the ISO.
Step 5: Tap the game to launch it.
Step 6: Go to Settings > Graphics. Set "Rendering Resolution" to 1x or 2x PSP (higher causes lag). Enable "Buffered Rendering" and "Hardware Transform".
Step 7: Enable "Frame Skipping" at 1 for smoother performance.
Step 8: A controller is highly recommended — wrestling games have a lot of inputs.

Pro Tips For Royal Rumble Success:
• Save your finishers for when new wrestlers enter — eliminate them quickly.
• Stay near the ropes — Irish whip opponents over the top rope.
• In Hell in a Cell, use the cell walls to damage your opponent.
• WarGames requires teamwork — keep switching with your partner.
• If the game stutters, turn down "Rendering Resolution" to 1x PSP.
• Roman Reigns' spear is the best finisher for quick eliminations in the Royal Rumble.

MyRise Career Mode — Your Path to WrestleMania

MyRise is WWE 2K24's career mode. You create your own wrestler (CAW) and choose your path. The mode has branching storylines — your choices affect your relationships with other wrestlers, your alignment (face or heel), and your opportunities. You can choose to fight for the WWE Championship, challenge for the Intercontinental title, or chase the tag team belts. The mode has multiple endings — depending on your decisions. The PSP port includes MyRise, though with fewer options than the console version. The mode is about 10-15 hours long.

Universe Mode — Book Your Own Shows

Universe mode is a booking simulator. You control a wrestling promotion — you can book shows (Raw, SmackDown, NXT), create rivalries, schedule pay-per-views, and simulate matches. You can also control the AI's decisions — you can let the computer book shows automatically, or manually control every match. The mode is endless — you can simulate years of wrestling history. The PSP port includes Universe mode, though with fewer options than the console version. The mode is addictive — you can spend hours just booking your dream WrestleMania card.
