Tag Team Mode — Double the Kombat

Mortal Kombat 9 introduced tag team mode — you choose two fighters and can switch between them during battle. You can also perform tag combos — juggle your opponent between characters for massive damage. The tag system adds strategic depth — you can use one character to zone and another to rush down. The PSP version includes tag team mode, though the frame rate can drop during tag combos. You can play tag team against the AI or with a friend via ad-hoc multiplayer. The challenge tower includes tag team missions — they're some of the hardest in the game.

Challenge Tower — 300 Missions of Mayhem

The challenge tower is one of Mortal Kombat 9's best features. 300 missions with unique conditions. Fight while blindfolded (the screen is black, you have to rely on sound). Survive lava floors (the ground burns you if you stand still). Fight two Goro clones at once. The missions are brutal but rewarding — completing them unlocks concept art, alternate costumes, and secret characters. The PSP version includes the challenge tower, though some missions are simplified due to hardware limitations. The compressed ISO preserves all 300 missions.
