The Roster — Classic Characters and Guest Fighters

The roster is packed. Returners include Scorpion, Sub-Zero, Liu Kang, Raiden, Johnny Cage, Sonya Blade, Kano, Mileena, Kitana, Jade, Ermac, Reptile, Noob Saibot, Baraka, D'Vorah, Kotal Kahn, and Shao Kahn. New characters include Geras (time-manipulating golem), Kollector (six-armed thief), and Cetrion (elder god of nature). Guest fighters include RoboCop (yes, that RoboCop), The Joker (DC Comics), Terminator (T-800), Rambo (John Rambo), and Spawn (Image Comics). The PSP port includes the full roster, though some guest characters may be missing due to licensing. The character models are lower resolution, but the moves are intact.

Gear System — Customize Your Fighter

Mortal Kombat 11 introduced the gear system — hundreds of skins, weapons, and abilities that change your fighter's appearance and stats. You can unlock gear through gameplay, the Krypt, or the Towers of Time. The gear system adds depth — you can create a Sub-Zero with ice-based abilities or a Scorpion with fire-based buffs. The PSP port includes the gear system, though the selection is limited compared to the console version. You can still unlock skins and abilities through gameplay. The customization options are fun to experiment with — nothing beats a neon-green Reptile with a top hat.

Story Mode — Time-Bending Epic

The story mode is the best in the series. Kronika, the keeper of time, is trying to reset history. Raiden and Liu Kang must unite the forces of good (and some villains) to stop her. The narrative spans multiple timelines — you fight as young versions of characters and old versions. The cutscenes are high-quality, with voice acting from the original cast. The story is engaging, with twists, deaths, and a satisfying conclusion. The PSP port preserves the full story mode, though the cutscenes are compressed. The dialogue is intact — Johnny Cage's one-liners are as funny as ever.

Fatalities and Brutalities — Still Gruesome

Mortal Kombat 11 has the best fatalities in the series. Scorpion's "Chain Reaction" impales the opponent with a spear, then burns them alive. Sub-Zero's "Ice-Cution" freezes the opponent, then shatters them. Liu Kang's "Shaolin Trap" uses pressure points to explode the opponent's head. The fatalities are creative, brutal, and hilarious. The brutality system adds new ways to end matches — perform a specific combo to trigger an instant kill. The PSP port includes all the fatalities and brutalities, though the graphics are compressed. The inputs for fatalities are complex — practice in training mode before online matches.

Towers of Time — Endless Challenges

The Towers of Time mode offers endless challenges. Each tower has modifiers — lava floors, random projectile rains, enemy buffs. The modifiers are annoying but add variety. The towers reward gear, skins, and currency. The PSP port includes the Towers of Time, though some modifiers are simplified. The AI difficulty spikes faster than Mileena's sais — be prepared to lose. The towers are the best way to unlock gear and skins. The game also includes a "Classic Towers" mode (arcade ladder) for those who want a traditional experience.
