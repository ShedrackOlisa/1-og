Campaign — The Cold War's Darkest Secrets

The single-player campaign is a highlight of the PSP's FPS library. You play as CIA operative Alex Mason, brainwashed by Soviet scientists as part of the MKUltra-style Nova Project. The narrative jumps between Vietnam War flashbacks, Soviet gulag escapes, and a secret Pentagon interrogation room. Each mission feels distinct: one moment you're piloting a helicopter gunship in "WMD," the next you're sneaking through a Russian shipyard in "Operation 40." The voice cast includes Sam Worthington as Mason, Gary Oldman as Reznov, and Ed Harris as Hudson — AAA talent that elevates the material. At just 350MB, the campaign offers 6-8 hours of gameplay with high replay value thanks to difficulty modes and hidden intel files.

Zombies Mode — Endless Undead Survival

Zombies mode steals the show. The PSP version includes "Kino der Toten" — the classic theater-based survival map from the console releases. You and up to three friends (via ad-hoc co-op) fight endless waves of undead Nazis, unlocking the mystery box for random weapons, activating traps, and pack-a-punching your gear. The Thundergun is as satisfying as ever — one blast clears a hallway. Hellhounds rounds keep you on your toes, and the Easter egg song (115 by Elena Siegman) creates an unforgettable atmosphere. On PPSSPP, the framerate holds steady even with 20+ zombies on screen. Four-player co-op via ad-hoc turns bus rides into undead-slaying marathons, though touchscreen accuracy makes the Thundergun spray feel like using a firehose in a hurricane — in the best way possible.

Multiplayer — Portable Call of Duty Action

The PSP version includes a pared-down but functional multiplayer mode. Six maps (including favorites like Firing Range and Summit), create-a-class with customizable perks and killstreaks, and ranked matches. Killstreaks like Spy Plane, Care Package, and Attack Helicopter work surprisingly well on handheld. Matchmaking is limited to ad-hoc local wireless, but you can play online via PPSSPP's community servers. The classic three-lane map design translates perfectly to the smaller screen, and matches are fast-paced — usually ending within 5-7 minutes. Don't expect Nuketown or the massive console map selection, but for portable Call of Duty, it's surprisingly addictive.

Weapons, Perks, and Equipment

The arsenal is impressive for a PSP game. Primary weapons include the M16, AK-47, FAMAS, and the iconic Commando. Secondary weapons cover pistols, shotguns, and launchers. Attachments like Red Dot Sight, Suppressor, and Extended Mags are available through multiplayer progression. Perks return from the console version: Lightweight increases movement speed, Sleight of Hand speeds up reloads, and Ninja silences your footsteps. Equipment includes frag grenades, semtex, flashbangs, and tomahawks. The create-a-class system gives you 10 custom slots, encouraging experimentation. Zombies mode adds Wonder Weapons like the Thundergun, Ray Gun, and Monkey Bombs — all faithfully recreated.
