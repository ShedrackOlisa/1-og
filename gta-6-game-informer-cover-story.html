GTA 6's Next Official Reveal Lands Tuesday: Here's What Game Informer's Cover Story Could Confirm

Published: September 27, 2026 by Oli Sa 6 min read
Fourteen pages. Twelve new screenshots. Interviews with Rockstar's lead designers. And a release date seven weeks out.

Rockstar Games has spent most of 2026 saying almost nothing about Grand Theft Auto VI. That changes Tuesday.

Game Informer confirmed on Thursday that GTA 6 is the cover story for Issue 382, and the details it has already put on the record are more substantial than anything Rockstar itself has volunteered since the second trailer. The digital edition goes live Tuesday, September 29. Print copies reach subscribers and newsstands over the following weeks.

The feature runs 14 pages and includes 12 exclusive screenshots that nobody outside Rockstar has seen. It was reported by senior associate editor Marcus Stewart, who interviewed several of Rockstar's lead designers. Stewart is a South Florida native. That is not an incidental detail; it is most of the reason this particular story exists in this particular form.

What Game Informer has already confirmed is in it

The magazine did something slightly unusual with its cover announcement: it told us the subject matter in advance. Three areas are named outright.

The weather system. Game Informer describes features built to simulate what it calls Florida's "less sunny" weather phenomena. Read that plainly and it means storms: the afternoon systems that roll in off the Gulf, and quite possibly hurricanes. Rockstar has shown rain in both trailers, but a magazine feature devoting space to a weather system implies something structural rather than cosmetic.

The wildlife. The feature covers what the magazine calls a staggering amount of wildlife, native and otherwise, roaming Leonida across land, air and sea. The "and otherwise" is doing real work in that sentence. Florida's invasive species problem, from pythons in the Everglades to iguanas dropping out of trees in cold snaps, is the kind of regional specificity Rockstar has built entire jokes around before.

The research. The third strand is how Rockstar studied Miami and the wider state to rebuild it as Leonida. This is the part Stewart's background makes worth reading. A writer who grew up there is going to notice whether the strip malls and the accents land, and he had Rockstar's designers on the phone to ask about it.

The cover itself

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos front the issue in original artwork commissioned from Rockstar, the pair posed in a car with weapons visible. It is the same visual language as the key art that has carried this campaign from the start, and it confirms what everyone assumed: this is Jason and Lucia's story, and Rockstar is not hiding a third playable character in the wings.

Why a magazine, and why now

Rockstar does not do press tours. It does not do hands-on previews eighteen months out. Historically it picks one outlet, hands over a controlled amount of access, and lets the resulting feature do the work of a dozen interviews. Game Informer has been that outlet before, repeatedly, going back through the Red Dead Redemption 2 and GTA 5 cycles.

So the choice of venue is not a surprise. The timing is the signal. GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026. A 14-page feature landing seven weeks out is the opening move of a marketing run, not a one-off. Expect the third trailer to follow, and expect it sooner rather than later.

It is worth remembering how long the road here has been. The game was tracking for a fall 2025 release until Rockstar pushed it in May 2025 to May 26, 2026. It moved again on November 6, 2025, alongside Take-Two's quarterly earnings, to the current November date. Rockstar's statement at the time was direct: "We are sorry for adding additional time to what we realize has been a long wait, but these extra months will allow us to finish the game with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve."

That November 19 date has held all year, and Take-Two has reaffirmed it on every earnings call since. A studio quietly worried about its date does not green-light a magazine cover story with screenshots seven weeks out.

What it probably will not tell you

Temper a few expectations. Cover stories built on studio access do not typically break news the studio would rather announce itself. Three things are unlikely to appear:

Price. Rockstar still has not confirmed what GTA 6 costs, and a magazine feature is not where that lands. That is an announcement with its own news cycle.

GTA Online's future. The single biggest open question for the existing player base, and exactly the sort of thing that gets held back for a dedicated beat.

Map scale. Rockstar has never handed out square-mileage figures ahead of launch and has no reason to start now.

What you will get is texture: how the world behaves, what lives in it, how much of the real Florida made it in. For a game this heavily scrutinised, that is not a small thing. It is the first substantive look at GTA 6 as a simulation rather than a trailer.

Seven weeks out

Tuesday is not a trailer, and it is not a release-date change. It is Rockstar opening the door slightly, on its own terms, through the outlet it has always used for this. Twelve new screenshots and confirmed detail on weather, wildlife and the research behind Leonida is the most concrete GTA 6 information anyone has had in months.

If the pattern from previous Rockstar campaigns holds, it is also the quietest thing that will happen between now and November 19.
