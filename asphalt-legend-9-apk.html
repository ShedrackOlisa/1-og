TouchDrive vs Manual — Which is Better?

Asphalt 9 introduced TouchDrive, a semi-automated steering system where you just swipe to choose routes and the game handles drifting and nitro. It's perfect for casual players or those who want to focus on the spectacle. However, for competitive racing and achieving the fastest lap times, manual mode is essential. Manual gives you full control over drifting angles, nitro management (single, double, or perfect nitro), and precision steering. The mod doesn't alter these systems, so you can switch anytime. We recommend manual for veterans and TouchDrive for newcomers — both are incredibly polished.
