Dream Team Mode: Build Your Ultimate Squad

The core mode of eFootball is Dream Team. You collect player cards, build your squad, and compete online against other players. What sets it apart from EA's Ultimate Team is the progression system, your players develop over time based on your coaching decisions. You can sign legendary players like classic icons and build hybrid teams across eras. The mode also features players from various leagues, including the newly added Liga Super Malaysia. The Dream Team (formerly MyClub) mode lets you sign managers, develop tactics, and compete in weekly events and challenges.

Gameplay: The Closest Thing to Real Football on Mobile

eFootball's gameplay is famously simulation-heavy. Passes have weight and inertia. Player positioning matters. Team chemistry and tactical familiarity affect performance. If you're used to arcade-style football games, there's a learning curve. But once it clicks, it's the most rewarding mobile football experience available. The 10.4.0 update continues refining the gameplay based on player feedback. Konami has been signing real-world managers as ambassadors, including José Mourinho and Rúben Amorim, who bring unique team playstyles to the game. The game also features match customization and Game Plan optimization.
