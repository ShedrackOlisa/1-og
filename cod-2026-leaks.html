Call of Duty 2026 Leaks: New Maps, Weapons & Warzone Updates You Must Know

Published: April 29, 2026 by Oli Sa 9 min read
"Season 03 Reloaded amps up the pace as players race against the clock in the new Freerun mode in Multiplayer and engage in high-speed chases across Avalon in Hot Pursuit" (Activision)

Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 and Warzone are gearing up for one of their most substantial mid-season updates yet. Season 3 Reloaded arrives April 30, 2026 at 9 AM PT, bringing a massive wave of new content across all modes . From a terrifying new Zombies map to high-octane Warzone modes and a surprising RoboCop crossover, here's everything you need to know about the biggest Call of Duty update of the year.

📅 Release Date
April 30, 2026
⏰ Time
9 AM PT / 12 PM ET
🎮 Mode
Mid-Season Reloaded
🏆 Event
RoboCop Crossover

Multiplayer: New Maps & Freerun Mode

The multiplayer suite is getting a significant injection of new content. Three maps are being added to the rotation, including one brand-new arena and two remastered fan favorites .

🎯 New Multiplayer Maps

  • Onsen A brand-new small-sized 6v6/2v2 map set in a scenic Japanese hot spring resort, designed for fast-paced, close-quarters combat.
  • Summit (Remastered) The classic snowy mountaintop facility from Black Ops returns, modernized with updated visuals.
  • Hacienda (Remastered) The luxurious estate map from Black Ops 4 makes its comeback.

Additionally, a completely new mode called Freerun is being introduced, a first-person obstacle course race where players compete using advanced movement mechanics like wall jumps, grappling hooks, and wingsuits across 10 different zones.

🔥 Limited-Time Modes

Two new LTMs are joining the rotation in Season 3 Reloaded:

  • Heat Wave Havoc Rewards players for team-based support actions with combat bonuses.
  • Freeze Tag A 6v6 twist on classic freeze tag where players freeze enemies and revive teammates.

Zombies: Totenreich – The Dark Aether Expands

Zombies fans are in for a treat. Season 3 Reloaded introduces Totenreich, a brand-new round-based map set in a Norwegian fishing village that was pulled into the Dark Aether . Players will uncover Group 935's dark experiments as they fight for survival.

🧟 Zombies Content

  • New Enemy: Necropincer, a terrifying new threat to test your squad.
  • New Wonder Weapon: Jotunn Star, a powerful mythical weapon unique to Totenreich.
  • New Field Upgrade: Wild Fire, deals massive area damage.
  • New quests, rewards, and Pack-a-Punch camos.

Warzone: Big Map Rotation, Hot Pursuit & Prop Hunt Royale

Warzone is undergoing a major shakeup with the introduction of big map rotation between Verdansk and Avalon in core Battle Royale. This marks the first time Warzone has supported multiple large-scale maps in a single playlist .

🌍 Map Rotation Details

Avalon, originally designed for Endgame mode, is now entering the standard Battle Royale rotation. The playlist swaps between Verdansk and Avalon every 10 minutes, giving players consistent access to both maps. Avalon is available exclusively in Quads at launch, though solo players can still drop in by disabling squad fill .

Verdansk has also received a major new POI, the Launch Facility adding fresh strategic options to the fan-favorite map .

🚗 Hot Pursuit Mode

The headlining LTM for Warzone is Hot Pursuit, a vehicle-focused Police vs. Thieves mode set on Avalon with 100 players .

  • Police team uses tactical vehicles like the JLTV and Light Heli to hunt down thieves.
  • Thieves rely on speed and evasion with UTVs and Dirt Bikes.
  • Uses Resurgence rules, teammates respawn as long as one squad member survives.

🎭 Prop Hunt Royale

The classic party mode makes its Warzone debut in a massive 24v24 format on Rebirth Island . Teams alternate between Props (hiding as objects) and Hunters (searching for disguised players). Decoys and Stun Grenades are available to Props; Hunters must rely on observation rather than explosives.

New Weapons & Equipment

The arsenal is expanding with two new weapons and new tactical equipment .

🎯 Siren Special Weapon ⚔️ Katana (Melee) 💥 Cluster Grenades (Expanded) ⚡ Spikes (Temporary Boosts)

📦 New Equipment: Spikes

Spikes are a new piece of tactical equipment offering temporary combat boosts to operators . These enhancements provide perks like armor salvaging and improved prone firing bonuses, adding a new layer of strategy to engagements.

Events: RoboCop Crossover

Perhaps the most unexpected addition is a RoboCop-themed event coming during the mid-season . Players can earn a RoboCop operator skin and exclusive cosmetic items through an event pass, alongside themed content updates.

Final Verdict: Season 3 Reloaded is shaping up to be the most content-packed mid-season update in Warzone history. From the terrifying Totenreich Zombies map to the chaotic vehicle battles of Hot Pursuit, there's something for every playstyle. The introduction of big map rotation finally addresses a long-standing community request, while the RoboCop crossover adds a fun, unexpected twist. Mark your calendars for April 30, this is one update you won't want to miss.
