Why FC 27, FC 26 and 2025 all appear

The headline is the only clear FC 27 label. The opening paragraph calls the package FC 26 PPSSPP, and the update list describes a 2025 transfer update. Until corrected, do not treat this post as proof of a 2026/27 roster or a distinct FC 27 PSP game.

PPSSPP instructions and missing details

The installation section says to place SAVEDATA and TEXTURE folders in the corresponding PSP directories and launch through PPSSPP. That points to emulator files, but the page does not clearly identify the PSP base game, required emulator version, or whether the download contains a game image, save data, textures, or some combination. Confirm the lawful base-game requirement and exact archive contents before setting anything up.

Practical advice: do not overwrite an existing PPSSPP save to test a package with uncertain identity. Back up the PSP/SAVEDATA and PSP/TEXTURE folders first, then test one change at a time.

What to verify before considering it

  • Ask which original PSP football game the update modifies.
  • Check the actual squad date and compare it with the “2027” headline.
  • Confirm whether the archive contains only save and texture data or additional game files.
  • Skip installers that hide file names or request permissions unrelated to gameplay.
