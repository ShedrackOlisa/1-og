Visual Overhaul — From 2004 to 2026

The difference is night and day. CJ's character model is completely redone — his cornrows look like actual hair, his clothes have fabric textures, his tattoos are sharp and detailed. The environments are unrecognizable — Grove Street's houses have realistic brick textures, the sidewalks have cracks and stains, the graffiti is crisp. The desert has actual Joshua trees and tumbleweeds. The forests have leaves that blow in the wind. The water effects are completely redone — waves actually look like waves now, with reflections and foam. The weather effects are stunning — proper rain, fog that rolls in from the ocean, and sunsets that make you want to take screenshots. The mod even includes a "NaturalVision" filter that makes the game look like a photography portfolio.

GTA 5 Features — Weaponized Vehicles and First-Person Mode

The mod integrates features from GTA 5. First-person mode is fully functional — drive in first-person, shoot in first-person, even fly in first-person. Weaponized vehicles have been added — the Oppressor Mk II (the flying motorcycle) is available, as well as the Insurgent and the Duke O'Death. The wanted system has been revamped — cops use tactics instead of psychic spawns, they set up roadblocks, and they coordinate their attacks. The driving physics have been tweaked to feel more like GTA 5 — cars have more weight, the handling is more responsive, and the crashes are more dramatic. The shooting has been improved with better aiming and cover mechanics.

New Content — Missions, Interiors, and Random Events

The mod includes new side missions and content. Track serial killers in Back O' Beyond. Run drug routes for the Aztecas. Gamble high-stakes poker in a remastered Four Dragons Casino. The map expands with new interiors — crack dens, police evidence rooms, and hidden bunkers. Random events feel next-gen — AI gang members actually retaliate for turf invasions instead of respawning like robots. The mod even includes some restored cut content — missions that Rockstar removed before release are back, fully playable. The modders didn't just improve the graphics — they added meaningful content.

Installation Guide

This mod is demanding. Make sure your device meets the requirements:

Step 1: Download the APK and OBB files using the buttons above (APK ~57MB, OBB ~1.6GB).
Step 2: Enable "Install from unknown sources" in Settings > Security.
Step 3: Install the APK — do NOT open the game yet!
Step 4: Extract the OBB folder and copy it to Internal Storage/Android/obb/.
Step 5: Launch the game. The first boot will take several minutes.
Step 6: Go to Graphics Settings and set quality to "High" (if your device can handle it).
Step 7: Enable "NaturalVision" filter in the display menu for the best visuals.

Performance Tips — This Mod is Demanding:
• A Snapdragon 865 or better is recommended for high settings.
• The mod needs 8GB of RAM to run smoothly. Lower-end devices may experience lag.
• Turn down "Shadow Quality" and "Reflection Quality" if the game stutters.
• The "NaturalVision" filter looks great but impacts performance — disable it on older devices.
• Close all background apps before playing — this mod needs as much resources as possible.
• The Oppressor Mk II is fun but can cause physics glitches. Save before using it.

The voice acting is the same. But watching CJ confront Big Smoke in a burning crack palace has never looked better. The mod respects the original's soul while sanding off the rough edges.
