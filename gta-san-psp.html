CJ's Story — Still the Best in the Series

For my money, San Andreas has the best story in GTA history. CJ is a relatable protagonist — he's not a super-soldier or a psychopath. He's just a guy trying to protect his family and his neighborhood. The story takes you from the gang-infested streets of Los Santos to the quiet suburbs of San Fierro to the glitzy casinos of Las Venturas. The voice cast is incredible — Samuel L. Jackson as Officer Tenpenny, James Woods as Mike Toreno, and Young Maylay as CJ. The PSP version preserves all the cutscenes and dialogue, though some are compressed. The emotional beats still hit — the death of CJ's brother, the betrayal of Big Smoke, the final confrontation with Tenpenny.

The PSP Version — What's Different

Let's be honest. The PSP isn't as powerful as the PS2. The San Andreas port has compromises. The map is slightly smaller — some countryside areas are cut. The draw distance is shorter — buildings pop in as you approach. The traffic density is lower. Some side activities are missing — no casino heists, no burglary missions. The frame rate can drop during intense police chases. But given the hardware limitations of the PSP, it's amazing the port exists at all. The core experience — the story, the gang warfare, the vehicle customization — is intact. And on PPSSPP, with resolution upscaling and texture filtering, it looks better than the original PSP version.

Gang Warfare and Territory Control

One of the best features of San Andreas is the gang warfare system. You can recruit homies, drive through enemy territory, and start shootouts. Taking over territory gives you income, respect, and weapons. The PSP version includes the territory system, though the map is smaller. You can still wage war against the Ballas and Vagos, still take over neighborhoods, still defend your turf. Recruiting gang members is simple — aim at a homie and press the recruit button. They're not the brightest AI, but they're useful for distracting enemies. The gang wars add a strategic layer to the game — you're not just completing missions, you're building an empire.

Step 5: Tap the game to launch it.
Step 6: Go to Settings > Graphics. Set "Rendering Resolution" to 2x or 3x PSP for sharper visuals. Enable "Buffered Rendering" and "Hardware Transform".

Customization — CJ Your Way

San Andreas is famous for its character customization. You can change CJ's clothes, hairstyle, tattoos, and even body shape. Hit the gym to build muscle, or eat too much and get fat. The PSP version includes most customization options — clothing stores are open, barbers are available, and the gym works. Some clothing options are missing, and the tattoo selection is smaller, but the core system is there. Customizing CJ adds personality to the game — you can make him look like a gangster, a businessman, or a clown. The game also tracks your "respect" level — the more respect you have, the more gang members follow you.

Weapons and Vehicles — Everything You Need

The weapon arsenal is classic San Andreas. Pistol, shotgun, SMG, AK-47, M4, sniper rifle, rocket launcher, flamethrower, and the chainsaw. The PSP version includes all the weapons, though some animations are simplified. The vehicle selection is impressive — cars, bikes, boats, planes, helicopters, and the jetpack. Yes, the jetpack is in the PSP version. Steal a police car, a taxi, or a fire truck for side missions. The vehicle physics are arcadey but fun — drifting around corners and causing massive pileups never gets old.
