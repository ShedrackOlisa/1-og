The Bound System: Extended Combos

The Bound system is new to Tekken 6. When you hit an opponent with a specific move while they're in the air, they bounce off the ground, allowing you to extend your combo. The system adds creative combo routes, you can launch, bound, juggle, and wall carry. The damage potential is massive, you can deal 50% or more with a single combo.
