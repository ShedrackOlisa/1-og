GTA 6 Review (Early Access): Graphics, Gameplay & Release Expectations in 2026

Published: April 29, 2026 by Oli Sa 12 min read
"GTA 6 will be the biggest, most immersive evolution of the Grand Theft Auto series yet." — Rockstar Games

After nearly 14 years since the release of Grand Theft Auto V, the gaming world is finally on the verge of experiencing the next generation of chaos, storytelling, and open-world freedom. I was lucky enough to go hands-on with an early access build of Grand Theft Auto VI, and I'm here to break down everything you need to know. From the jaw-dropping graphics powered by a $1+ billion budget to the completely redesigned wanted system, here is my full review of what is shaping up to be the most ambitious game ever made.

Graphics & Presentation: A Generational Leap Forward

The first thing that hits you when you load into Leonida (GTA 6's fictional take on Florida) is the visual fidelity. Rockstar has reportedly hired a separate team of 20 engineers specifically to work on water physics — and it shows . The waves in Vice City don't just look good; they behave like real fluid dynamics. Boats create genuine wakes that affect the shoreline, and even small details like the fizzing bubbles in a beer bottle are fully simulated .

However, it's worth noting the ongoing debate about the final game's quality. Some fans have scrutinized the trailers, suggesting that the visuals might not be entirely representative of the final gameplay performance on standard PS5s . While the trailer footage was captured on PS5, concerns remain about whether the game can maintain such high fidelity without stability issues. If Rockstar's history with Red Dead Redemption 2 is any indicator, the final product will likely look even better than the trailers due to continued polishing .

Gameplay Mechanics: Smarter AI & A Brutal New Wanted System

Gameplay is where GTA 6 truly differentiates itself. The most significant overhaul is the wanted system. Gone are the days of simply driving out of a circular radius to lose the cops .

Dynamic Search Zones: Police now create a huge, dynamic search perimeter. They will use ground units and helicopters to sweep the area. Hiding in an alley won't work if they saw you run in there.
Witness & Description Systems: Law enforcement now relies on witness reports. If you commit a crime in a remote area with no witnesses, you might get away clean. However, if the cops get a description of your character (your clothes, your car, your face), they will actively look for those specific traits. You will need to change your clothes or respray your vehicle to fully escape .

Additionally, the NPC AI has received a massive upgrade. Traffic is no longer on rails. In my playthrough, I watched an NPC motorcyclist physically reposition his body to look for oncoming traffic before illegally overtaking a slow truck. This dynamic behavior, likely powered by Rockstar's new "Black Box" data-sharing technology, makes the world feel unpredictably alive .

Story & Characters: A Modern Bonnie and Clyde

The narrative follows Lucia and Jason, a duo described as "partners in crime." Lucia hails from Liberty City, while Jason appears to be an ex-military man, likely a paratrooper based on his tattoos .

Without spoiling the early missions I played, the chemistry between the two is electric. It feels grittier and more grounded than GTA V's trio. The dialogue is sharp, and the game allows you to switch between the two on the fly during missions, allowing for creative tactical approaches to robberies and heists.

The Good

  • Stunning next-gen graphics and lighting.
  • Revamped wanted system adds real tension.
  • Incredible NPC AI and traffic behavior.
  • Massive, dense world with 700+ interiors.
  • Dynamic water physics are a technical marvel.

The Bad

  • Requires a PS5 Pro or high-end hardware for 60fps.
  • GTA Online progress resets completely.
  • Violence realism may be "too much" for some players.
  • No PC version planned for launch window.

Release Expectations: Is November 19th locked?

Given Rockstar's history of delays, the gaming community has been holding its breath. However, recent reports from industry insiders suggest that the November 19, 2026 release date is firmly on track .

Take-Two Interactive CEO Strauss Zelnick recently reassured fans that marketing will begin to ramp up "soon," with a heavy push expected during the summer of 2026 . For those worried about storage, prepare your SSDs; the game size is expected to exceed 150GB.

Final Verdict: Worth the wait?

I walked away from my early access session feeling tired—but in the best way possible. GTA 6 respects your intelligence. It punishes reckless behavior with the new police system but rewards creativity. The world of Leonida is packed with secrets (over 700 accessible buildings according to leaks) and feels like a living, breathing parody of modern America .

The realism might be jarring for some. As the BBC recently pointed out, the violence is getting so lifelike that it enters a "murky" territory . However, for those seeking the absolute pinnacle of open-world gaming, GTA 6 is a masterpiece in progress. If Rockstar nails the optimization and delivers stable framerates on standard consoles, this will easily be the Game of the Year and likely the best-selling entertainment product of all time.

Verdict: A monumental technical achievement. Suffers from high hardware requirements, but delivers an unmatched open-world experience.
