Xbox Game Pass April 2026 Games List: Full Lineup & Surprise Additions

Published: April 29, 2026 by Oli Sa 12 min read
"April is turning out to be a strong month for Xbox Game Pass users." (Notebookcheck)

Microsoft has loaded Xbox Game Pass with one of its busiest months of the year. April 2026 brings a massive wave of new titles, including long-awaited sequels, major remasters, sports updates, and a surprising number of smaller indie releases. With over 40 games added throughout the month, including day-one launches like Hades II, Replaced, and Trepang2 this might be the most stacked Game Pass update since the service began.

Headliners You Don't Want to Miss

Hades II: April 14 (Day One)

The long-awaited sequel to Supergiant Games' award-winning roguelike finally makes its console debut, launching directly into Game Pass on day one. Building on the original's formula with new gods, weapons, and a larger map, Hades II is arguably the biggest title of the month. The game follows Melinoë, the immortal Princess of the Underworld, as she battles the Titan of Time .

The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion Remastered, April 16

Bethesda's 2006 classic returns with modernized visuals, updated gameplay systems, and all its expansions. The remaster brings quality-of-life improvements to the beloved RPG, while maintaining the charm of the original. If you've never explored Cyrodiil, this is the perfect time to start .

Trepang2: April 29

This high-intensity FPS is being compared to classic FEAR for its slow-motion mechanics, brutal combat, and fast movement. It's a gory, action-packed shooter set in the near future that rewards aggressive play .

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare (2019), April 17

The 2019 reboot of Modern Warfare joins Game Pass with its full campaign, multiplayer, and co-op Special Ops modes. It's the game that revitalized Call of Duty's campaign storytelling and introduced the Gunsmith system .

Day-One Surprises & Indie Highlights

Beyond the headliners, April brings a strong selection of day-one indie titles :

  • Replaced (April 14) A 2.5D cyberpunk platformer with pixel-art visuals and a deep narrative.
  • Vampire Crawlers (April 21) A fast-paced rogue-lite card game inspired by Vampire Survivors.
  • Kiln (April 23) A weird and wonderful multiplayer brawler where you craft pottery and smash opponents with it.
  • Little Rocket Lab (April 21) A cozy machine-building RPG about helping a community build a rocket.
  • Sopa: Tale of the Stolen Potato (April 21) A magical narrative adventure set in a South American-inspired world.

Games Leaving Game Pass: April 30 & April 15 Deadlines

As always, some games are rotating out. April 30 is the major departure date for titles from the second wave, but note that several high-profile games left earlier in the month .

Game Updates & Live Service Content

Two notable updates arrived in April as well :

  • Dome Keeper, Multiplayer Update (April 13): The popular mining roguelike now supports co-op.
  • Call of Duty: Black Ops 7, Season 03 (April): New maps, modes, weapons, and the Avalon map rotation in Warzone.

Final Verdict: April 2026 Might Be the Best Month Yet

Between the console debut of Hades II, the remaster of Oblivion, the brutal shooter Trepang2, and a slew of promising indie day-one launches, Xbox Game Pass has delivered arguably its strongest month of 2026. The variety is impressive: roguelikes, RPGs, strategy games, cozy sims, and intense shooters all have a place.

If you haven't played Citizen Sleeper or Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 yet, you have until April 30 to finish them. And if you missed GTA V when it left on April 15... well, that one might sting a bit. Overall, this is a month that fully justifies the subscription cost .
