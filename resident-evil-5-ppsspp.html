Co-op Mode: Best Played With a Friend

Resident Evil 5 is best played with a friend. The co-op mode supports two players via ad-hoc (local wireless). You can play as Chris and Sheva, coordinating attacks and sharing items. The co-op mechanics are well-designed, you can revive each other, trade weapons, and perform team melee attacks. The PSP port includes co-op, though you'll need two devices and two copies of the ISO. The co-op is chaotic but fun, nothing beats suplexing a Majini while your partner shoots its legs. The AI partner (if you play solo) is improved in this port, Sheva conserves ammo and actually helps in battle.

Combat: Punching Boulders and Suplexing Zombies

The combat is action-focused. You can move and shoot (unlike RE4), use cover, and perform melee attacks. The melee attacks are satisfying, Chris's straight punch, Sheva's roundhouse kick, and the suplex. The weapons are classic, handguns, shotguns, rifles, grenades, and magnums. The upgrade system returns, you can upgrade weapons between missions. The PSP port preserves the combat system, though the controls are simplified. The infamous boulder-punching scene is included, yes, you still have to mash the button to punch a boulder. It's ridiculous, but it's part of the charm.

The Story: Wesker's Final Act

The story is the conclusion of the Chris/Wesker rivalry. Wesker is trying to unleash Uroboros, a virus that turns people into tentacle monsters. Chris and Sheva chase him through Africa, the marshlands, and an underground laboratory. The story is over-the-top but entertaining. The dialogue is cheesy, "I'm not going to lose anyone else!" The PSP port preserves the full story, including the cutscenes. The ending is satisfying, Wesker's defeat is cathartic.

Mercenaries Mode: Arcade Score Attack

Mercenaries mode returns from RE4. You fight waves of enemies within a time limit, earning points for kills and combos. The roster includes Chris, Sheva, Wesker, Jill, and other characters. Each character has unique weapons and abilities. The PSP port includes Mercenaries mode, though the controls are simplified. Unlocking all characters requires high scores, it's a challenge, but worth it. The Mercenaries mode is addictive, it's perfect for short play sessions.
