2027 Football Games for Android: FTS 27, DLS 27, FIFA Mods & More

Updated: October 2026 by Oli Sa
Several Android football updates are already being labeled for the 2026/27 season. AspectGaming’s September 2026 homepage lists FTS 27, DLS 27, FIFA 16 FC 27 mods and eFootball PES 2027 PPSSPP. These are listings from a third-party site; season labels on fan-made patches do not mean the games are official releases or that every advertised feature has been independently confirmed.

Here is a quick guide to the football titles currently surfaced on AspectGaming. The source homepage provides titles and short descriptions, while some post pages were unavailable during research. For that reason, this guide sticks to the claims visible in the listings and avoids treating them as verified download specifications.

FTS 27 Mobile

AspectGaming lists FTS 27 Mobile as an “Original Season Update” with kits and 26/27 transfers, plus graphics, faces and grass updates. This is described as an update to the community-modded First Touch Soccer experience. It is not an official new release from the original developers. Check the exact build, file requirements and device compatibility on the source page before installing.

View AspectGaming’s FTS 27 listing or read our FTS 27 source review. OlitechGames also has background on the prior season in our FTS 26 article.

DLS 27 (Dream League Soccer 19 mod)

The site lists DLS 19 Mod DLS 27, describing it as a 2027-season update with transfers and HD graphics. The title makes clear that this is presented as a mod built on DLS 19, rather than a new official Dream League Soccer release. AspectGaming’s title mentions APK, OBB and data files; verify the archive contents, version and installation steps at the source.

View AspectGaming’s DLS 27 listing or read our DLS 27 source review. See also our DLS 2026 article for the earlier season edition.

FIFA 16 Mod FC 27

AspectGaming’s homepage features a FIFA 16 Mod FC 27 Android offline update and advertises real faces, 26/27 transfers and HD graphics. Treat “FC 27” as the mod’s season theme: EA has not announced this third-party patch as an official FIFA or EA SPORTS FC product. The source homepage shows more than one FC 27 FIFA 16 post, so compare dates and build details before choosing a version.

View the newer FIFA 16 FC 27 listing or the big-update listing. Read our FIFA 16 FC 27 source review for the conflicting season details. Our site’s related prior-season coverage: FIFA 16 Mod FIFA 26.

eFootball PES 2027 PPSSPP

AspectGaming lists an eFootball PES 2027 PPSSPP season update for the PSP emulator, with the listing title mentioning offline play, real faces, transfers and HD graphics. These labels describe a community update for a PSP football game, not a separate official Konami Android release. Confirm which base game and emulator files are required before downloading.

View the PES 2027 PPSSPP listing or read our PES 2027 source review. Existing OlitechGames coverage: eFootball PES 2026.

EA Sports FC 27 PPSSPP

The homepage also lists EA Sports FC 27 PPSSPP. The article page available during research is internally inconsistent: its headline says FC 27, but its body calls the package FC 26 and describes a 2025 transfer update. That mismatch means its stated season and features need verification before relying on them.

Read the FC 27 PPSSPP post or see our source mismatch review. The source page’s own text names an FC 26 package and 2025 updates, so check for a corrected release.

What about DFL 2027?

I could not find a DFL 2027 listing on AspectGaming’s homepage or in its visible Sports Games archive when checked on October 2, 2026. OlitechGames does cover the earlier DFL 2026 FIFA 16 mod, but I can’t confirm a 2027 edition from the pages available. I’ll update this guide if AspectGaming publishes a matching DFL post with verifiable details.

Before you install a fan-made football update

  • Check whether the page identifies the original base game, mod version and file requirements.
  • Read the installation instructions and confirm your device has enough storage.
  • Be cautious with files that request unrelated permissions or are hosted behind confusing download buttons.
  • Use official app stores for official releases; fan-made season patches are unofficial and may stop working after updates.

Source checked October 2, 2026: AspectGaming homepage and the linked game posts. Feature descriptions above are attributed to AspectGaming and have not been independently tested by OlitechGames.
