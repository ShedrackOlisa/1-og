Naughty Dog Delays Its Last of Us Day Reveals to Monday, and the Tease Suggests Partnerships, Not Part 3

Published: September 27, 2026 by Oli Sa 5 min read
"Expect a partnership announcement, a very cool collaboration, digital surprises, and more." (Naughty Dog)

The Last of Us Day came and went on Saturday without the announcement a lot of people were waiting for. Naughty Dog is doing it Monday instead, and it has already told everyone, in fairly plain language, what is coming.

The studio confirmed it is holding its #TLOUDay festivities until Monday, September 28, rather than September 26, the in-fiction Outbreak Day the community has marked since 2013. In its place on Saturday, Naughty Dog put out a free animated background for PS5 built from The Last of Us Part I and Part II Remastered artwork, featuring Ellie, Joel and Abby. Nice gesture. Not an announcement.

Here is the studio's own wording for Monday: a partnership announcement, a very cool collaboration, digital surprises, and more, plus a No Return charity stream on Twitch.

Read that list again

Studios choose these words carefully, and none of the words Naughty Dog chose are the ones you use to announce a game.

"Partnership announcement" is brand language. It describes an arrangement with another company: apparel, collectibles, a hardware tie-in, a crossover into somebody else's live-service title. "Collaboration" points the same direction. "Digital surprises" is the category the PS5 background from Saturday already fits into: avatars, themes, in-game cosmetics, maybe a discount window.

When a studio has a game to announce, it says so, or it says nothing at all and lets a trailer do the talking. It does not pre-announce a sequel as a "partnership announcement."

Where Part 3 actually stands

Neil Druckmann has been consistent, and consistently misread. He has not ruled out The Last of Us Part III. He has said Naughty Dog is not finished with the property. What he has not said is that it is close.

By the accounts available, a third entry sits in very early development, and the studio's hands are full. Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet, announced at The Game Awards in December 2024 with Druckmann as creative director, is reportedly targeting 2027 and still has no dated release. There is a new Uncharted in the works as well. That is a studio with its next several years spoken for.

So the realistic ceiling for Monday is not Part 3. It is something adjacent to the franchise that keeps it visible while the actual games are years out.

The HBO side is where the news already is

If you want confirmed Last of Us news from this week, it came from the television side rather than the studio. Season 3 of the HBO adaptation has added John Goodman, Ian Alexander and Laura Bailey to its cast.

Goodman and Alexander play Joey and Paco, two new characters described as "Heroes of the Highway." Bailey, who played Abby in Part II and absorbed a genuinely ugly wave of harassment for it, takes the role of Elizabeth, leader of the Seraphite religious sect. That is a pointed and rather elegant bit of casting, and it tells you Season 3 is heading deep into Seraphite territory.

What Monday probably looks like

Set expectations at the level Naughty Dog set them. A brand partnership, most likely physical merchandise or a crossover. Something digital and free or cheap. The No Return charity stream, which is the part most worth your time and the only part with a stated purpose beyond marketing.

There is one caveat worth keeping. Studios do occasionally bury a real announcement at the end of a list that reads like merchandise, and "and more" is carrying weight in that sentence. But betting on it means betting against every word Naughty Dog actually used.

Where that leaves things

The Last of Us Day 2026 moved to Monday, September 28. The studio promised a partnership, a collaboration, digital surprises and a charity stream, and that list is the story. Anyone reading a Part 3 reveal into it is reading something that is not on the page.

Tune in for the charity stream. Treat anything beyond that as upside.
