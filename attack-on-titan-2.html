Character Creation and Bonding System

Unlike the first game where you played as existing characters, Attack on Titan 2 lets you create your own scout. Customize appearance, voice, and backstory, then join the 104th Training Corps alongside Eren, Mikasa, Armin, Jean, and Connie. The bond system tracks your relationships, share meals, have conversations, and complete joint missions to raise affinity. Higher affinity unlocks special abilities, combo attacks, and even romantic story branches. Your choices affect dialogue and who survives certain story beats, adding replayability. It's a brilliant way to insert you into the narrative without breaking canon.

Weapons and Gear Upgrades

The Survey Corps issue standard gear, but you can upgrade every component. Blades come in different sharpness levels, higher sharpness deals more damage but breaks faster. Gas canisters affect boost duration and speed. Anchor wires have varying tensile strength and range. The equipment system encourages specialization: build a speed-focused scout for hit-and-run attacks, or a tanky soldier who can survive grabs. The workshop lets you craft rare materials obtained from taking down boss titans. Mastering gear management is essential for the harder difficulty modes, where titans regenerate health and move faster.
