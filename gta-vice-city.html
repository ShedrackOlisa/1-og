Vice City: Neon Soaked Perfection

Vice City is a love letter to Miami. The map isn't huge, but it's dense with personality. Ocean Drive with its neon lights. The beaches with their bright sand. The Cuban gang strongholds of Little Havana. The glitzy mansions of Starfish Island. The seedy underbelly of Vice Point. The graphics are cleaned up for mobile, though some textures look fuzzy on modern screens. But the atmosphere is timeless, driving down Ocean Drive at sunset with "Billie Jean" playing is a gaming moment I'll never forget. The mobile version includes all the radio stations, Flash FM, V-Rock, Emotion 98.3, and the hilarious talk radio on K-Chat.

The weapons are classic, pistol, shotgun, SMG, assault rifle, sniper, flamethrower, chainsaw, and the rocket launcher. The vehicle selection is perfect, sports cars, speedboats, helicopters, and the tank (cheat code required). A controller is recommended for the best experience, but the touch controls are serviceable.
