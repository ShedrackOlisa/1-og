Heat Smash System: New Mechanic

Tekken 8 introduces the Heat Smash system, a new mechanic that rewards aggressive play. When you activate Heat mode, your character glows red, deals more damage, and gains new moves. The Heat Smash is a powerful attack that consumes your Heat gauge. The mobile port includes the Heat Smash system, though the effects are simplified.
